Description

Columbina and Sandrone hold hands under a huge full moon, faces close together in the dark. Columbina, blindfolded in white bands with magenta-streaked dark hair, smiles with her eyes closed; Sandrone, blushing, looks down at their joined hands, her blonde hair framed by a white maid headdress with gold trim and a red gem choker at her throat.

Thin white threads cross the whole frame — a quiet nod to Sandrone the Marionette — while a single white wing rises behind Columbina and the moon’s glow rims their hair in silver. The palette is deep night blue against warm moonlight and skin tones, with soft painterly shading, and the artist’s signature sits in the lower corner.

Rendered in a tall 801×1228 portrait, this one is made for a phone screen. The dark sky keeps your lockscreen clock legible, the moon sits high in the frame behind it, and the deep blues look silky on OLED — though at 801 pixels wide it is softer than the other two, so expect a little upscaling on larger phones.
