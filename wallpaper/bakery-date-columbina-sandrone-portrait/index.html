Description

Columbina and Sandrone — the ‘Sandbina’ pair — arm in arm outside a bakery, drawn in a school-uniform alternate setting. Columbina, blindfolded in white crisscross bands with a gold crescent hairpin and small white wings, flashes a peace sign with a soft smile; Sandrone, blushing, holds her phone and looks straight at you, her ash-blonde hair tied with a big white bow.

Both wear cardigans over collared shirts with big plaid bow ties, plaid skirts and thighhighs, school bags slung over their shoulders. A plate of cream-filled crepes sits on a table in the foreground, pastry cases full of cakes and buns glow behind them, and brick, ivy and a pot of yellow flowers frame the scene. Warm sunlight and soft polished cel shading keep the whole thing bright and gentle.

Rendered in a tall 1600×2178 portrait, this one is made for a phone screen. The warm, bright palette pops on a lockscreen, the pair sit centered so your clock sits over the bright bakery glass above them, and the sunny colours suit a light home screen.
